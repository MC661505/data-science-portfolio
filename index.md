###Project Title & Research Question  
**Project Title**：Are Macao House Prices Actually Running Away from What People Earn? A Look at Housing Affordability from 2013 to 2025  
**Research question**：Has the housing price in Macao outpaced the income of its residents over the past decade? If so, by how much?
### Visualization and brief explanation 
![Comparison Chart of Housing Prices and Income Growth](assets/price_income_gap.png)  

**Explanation**：  
-This graph illustrates the trend of the residential price index (blue line) and the resident monthly income index (orange line) after being normalized by a 100-point benchmark. The residential prices have grown at a faster rate, resulting in a continuously widening cumulative gap between the two, indicating a decline in the affordability of housing (as described above ).
